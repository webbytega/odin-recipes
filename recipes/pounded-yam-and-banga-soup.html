Pounded yam and Banga soup

This is a native soup popular in the South-South side of Nigeria, Delta state to be precise.

It pairs the stretchy texture of pounded white yam with a rich palm fruit extract soup.

Ingredients

  • White Yam: Fresh tubers peeled, boiled, and pounded smooth.
  • Banga Extract: Liquid squeezed from boiled palm fruits or canned puree.
  • Banga Spices: A blend of ataiko, irugege, and aidan fruit.
  • Herbs: Fresh scent leaves (efirin) or dried beletientien leaves.
  • Proteins: Catfish, dried fish, bushmeat, or assorted beef.

Steps

  1. Extract Palm Juice: Boil fresh palm fruits and pound them, or mix canned extract with warm water.
  2. Cook Proteins: Boil your seasoned meats and stockfish until they are halfway tender.
  3. Thicken Soup: Boil the palm fruit liquid until its oil rises to the top.
  4. Spice It: Stir in ground crayfish, fresh pepper, and the unique banga spice mix.
  5. Add Fish & Herbs: Simmer fresh catfish, periwinkles, and fresh scent leaves for 10 minutes.
  6. Make Swallow: Boil yam slices until soft, then pound thoroughly using a mortar or food processor.
