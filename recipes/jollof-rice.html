Jollof Rice

The Image above shows a plate of a popular Nigerian meal called Jollof rice and fried chicken. It has a sweet, smoky flavour that makes the rice tasty and when paired with chicken (and mostly Plantains and Salad), makes the dish even better.

Its my favourite meal because its healthy and quick to prepare.

Ingredients

  • Rice: 3 cups of long-grain parboiled rice
  • Blend: large red bell peppers (tatashe), tomatoes and scotch bonnet peppers (rodo)
  • Aromatics: 2 medium onions (chopped) and minced garlic and ginger
  • Base: 1 cup tomato paste
  • Chicken
  • Liquid: 2 cups meat stock (beef or chicken) and water
  • Spices: Curry powder, dried thyme, bay leaves, bouillon cubes, and salt
  • Vegetable oil and 2 tablespoons of butter

Instructions

  1. Blend and reduce: Blend the red bell peppers, tomatoes, and scotch bonnets with one onion. Pour the mix into a pot and boil it on medium heat until the water reduces
  2. Fry the paste: Heat oil in a large pot. Fry half of the sliced onions until fragrant. Add the tomato paste and fry on low-medium heat for 10 to 15 minutes until it darkens
  3. Add the pepper mix: Pour in the reduced pepper blend. Add curry powder, thyme, bay leaves, bouillon, and salt. Stir and fry for 10 minutes
  4. Combine stock and rice: Wash the rice until the water runs clear. Add the meat stock and the washed rice to the pot. The liquid level should sit just level with or slightly above the rice.
  5. Steam to cook: Cover the pot tightly with foil paper, then place the lid on top to trap the steam. Cook on low heat for 35 to 40 minutes.
  6. Finish: Stir in the butter for extra richness, fluff the rice, and serve warm with your fried chicken, paired with salad, fried plantains and a nice cold drink.
